# Arganista — product photo guide (phone)

Goal: one clean photo per product that I can turn into a professional studio shot for the shop.

## What you need
- Your phone (back camera, not selfie)
- A table near a large window, **daylight, no direct sun** (morning or late afternoon, or a cloudy day)
- A sheet of **white paper or white card** (A3 or bigger) bent like a curve: half flat on the table, half up against a wall or box (this is called a "sweep", it hides the corner)
- A soft cloth to clean the bottles

## Setting up
1. Place the white sweep on the table, next to the window (window on your **left or right**, never behind the product).
2. Clean the bottle: no fingerprints, no oil drips, the label straight.
3. Put **one product only** in the middle of the white paper. Turn it so the label faces you.
4. Lights in the room: turn them **off** (mixing yellow lamps with daylight changes the colours).

## Taking the photo
- Hold the phone at the **height of the middle of the product**, straight, not from above.
- Stand about 40–60 cm away and **zoom 2×** (less distortion, looks more professional).
- Leave some white space all around the product. Don't cut the cap or the bottom.
- Tap on the label to focus; slide the exposure a little **down** if the white paper looks grey-blue or burnt.
- Don't use the flash. Don't use filters or "portrait" mode.
- Take 3–4 photos of each, pick the sharpest.

## Which photos
For each product, the label facing forward:
- Huile d'argan cosmétique: **30 ml** and **100 ml** (one photo each)
- Huile de pépins de figue de barbarie: **15 ml** and **30 ml**
- Sérum anti-rides à la rose · Sérum anti-âge
- Crème visage à la rose · Crème anti-rides argan & rose (**one jar**, lid on)
- Gel nettoyant visage · Shampoing argan & fleur d'oranger
- Pâte ghassouline à la rose · à la fleur d'oranger (**one jar**)
- Huile d'argan alimentaire: **250 ml, 500 ml, 1 L**
- Amlou beldi (one jar)

Bonus (optional, makes the site richer):
- One photo of each product **open**: dropper with a drop of oil, cream texture on a finger, ghassoul paste in a small bowl.
- A few photos of the **cooperative** (hands cracking argan nuts, the press, the women at work), with their permission.
